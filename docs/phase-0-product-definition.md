# MyCloset — Phase 0: Product Definition

## 1. Project Overview

**MyCloset** is a smart wardrobe assistant designed primarily for university students.

The goal is simple:

> **Look better with what you already have.**

MyCloset helps users make better outfits from the clothes they already own instead of feeling the need to constantly purchase new clothes.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

University students often own a reasonable amount of clothing but struggle with deciding what to wear.

They may:

- Have difficulty combining different clothing items.
- Repeatedly wear the same outfits.
- Buy new clothes because they feel they have "nothing to wear."
- Struggle to dress appropriately for different occasions.
- Want to look stylish while working within a limited budget.

MyCloset aims to solve this by helping students discover outfit combinations using their existing wardrobe.

---

## 3. Target Users

### Primary Target Audience

**University students**

The initial version of MyCloset will focus on university students because they commonly need outfits for different situations while also being conscious of their clothing budget.

Typical situations may include:

- University classes
- Presentations
- Casual hangouts
- University events
- Formal events
- Interviews
- Everyday activities

The target audience may be expanded in future versions.

---

## 4. Core Value Proposition

MyCloset's core promise is:

> **"Look better with what you already have."**

Instead of encouraging users to buy more clothes, MyCloset focuses on helping them make better use of their existing wardrobe.

---

## 5. Product Vision

The long-term vision is to create an intelligent personal stylist that understands a user's wardrobe and helps them decide what to wear.

A user should eventually be able to ask something like:

> "What should I wear to my university presentation tomorrow?"

MyCloset should then consider the user's available clothing, occasion, weather, color combinations, personal style, and previous outfits before recommending suitable outfits.

---

## 6. Feature Vision

The long-term version of MyCloset will contain the following features.

### 6.1 Add Clothing Items

Users can add clothing items to their digital closet.

Each item will initially contain:

- Image
- Name
- Category
- Color
- Style
- Season
- Formality
- Pattern
